Aerie

A live 3D wallpaper for Pop!_OS COSMIC, like Wallpaper Engine: widgets, an app launcher and 3D objects on your desktop, with a camera that follows your mouse. Free, offline, no tracking.

Install

Open COSMIC Terminal and paste:

curl -fsSL https://jayswansocool48.github.io/aerie/install.sh | bash

Aerie then appears in your app list and in the COSMIC Store. Updates arrive with your other updates in the COSMIC Store.

Uninstall

Aerie → Settings → Uninstall Aerie, or remove it in the COSMIC Store.
