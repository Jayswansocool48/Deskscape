# Privacy policy

_Last updated: October 13, 2026 (version 1.0.0)_

Aerie runs on your computer. It only goes online for two optional features you turn on yourself: the design assistant (if you choose Claude or ChatGPT) and the online library.

- **No data is collected by us.** There are no accounts, sign-ups, analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, ads or tracking. We (the publisher) never receive anything from your computer.
- **Offline unless you use the assistant or the online library.** Aerie's screen is a page served by its own small server that only listens on `127.0.0.1` (your own computer) and needs a random key that changes every time Aerie starts. Updates are downloaded by Pop!_OS's normal updater / the COSMIC Store, not by Aerie.
- **Design assistant (optional, off until you set it up):** you choose which AI it uses.
  - **Ollama:** runs on your own computer. Nothing leaves it.
  - **Claude (Anthropic) or ChatGPT (OpenAI):** each time you send a message, Aerie sends that message, the conversation so far, and a short description of your current desktop design (background and colors, lighting, particles, widget types and positions, 3D shapes and their colors) to that company, using your own API key. Never sent: your notes, to-dos, names you typed into widgets, pictures, files, app list, or anything about your computer. That company's terms and privacy policy apply to those requests ([Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy), [OpenAI](https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy/)), and they may charge your account for use.
  - **Your API key** is saved only in `~/.config/aerie/assistant.json`, readable only by your user account, and is only ever sent to the service it belongs to. "Forget my key" removes it. Conversations are not saved; they're gone when you close the editor.
- **Online library (optional, off until you turn it on):** when you open it, or pick a preset with a 360° sky, Aerie asks first, then downloads the catalog, small previews and only the skies or models you choose from Poly Haven (`api.polyhaven.com`, `cdn.polyhaven.com`, `dl.polyhaven.org`). Poly Haven sees an ordinary download request: your internet address and that it comes from Aerie (its version). Nothing else about you, your files or your setup is sent. [Poly Haven's privacy policy](https://polyhaven.com/privacy) applies to those requests. You can turn it off in Settings → Online library; what you downloaded is kept in `~/.local/share/aerie/library` and keeps working offline.
- **What is stored, and where:** your setup (including text you type in the Notes widget) and any images, videos or 3D models you add, in `~/.config/aerie/` and `~/.local/share/aerie/`, plus a small log in `~/.cache/aerie.log`. If you turn on "Start when I log in", one file is added: `~/.config/autostart/io.github.jayswansocool48.Aerie.desktop`.
- **Also stored, in your setup file:** the apps you pin to the dock and the last 12 apps you opened from Aerie (for "Recently used" sorting), your to-do items, and looks you save (with a small picture of your desktop at that moment, kept in `~/.local/share/aerie/media/`). Sound files you choose are copied there too and never leave your computer.
- **Your windows:** to pause while you use apps, Aerie reads which windows are maximized, full screen, focused or minimized, and on which screen and workspace. It doesn't read window titles or contents, and none of this is stored.
- **What it reads, without storing it:** the list of installed apps and their icons (for the app widgets); CPU, memory, disk, battery and network speed (for the System monitor and graph widgets); and what your music or video player is playing (for the Now playing widget, and to fade the ambient soundscape out while music plays). These are only shown on your screen.
- **Changes to COSMIC:** only if you use the COSMIC tab or agree to a preset's matching taskbar. This can change COSMIC's glass, window, panel and dock settings, which applets your panel and dock show, and your pinned apps. Aerie saves your original COSMIC settings in `~/.local/share/aerie/cosmic-backup.json` first and puts them back when you click Restore, delete your data, or uninstall.
- **Profile picture:** if you change it from the Lock screen section, Aerie hands the picture to your system's account service (AccountsService, as COSMIC Settings does) and keeps a copy of your previous picture in `~/.local/share/aerie/` so Restore can put it back.
- **Lock screen:** if you set it from the COSMIC tab, the picture is saved in `~/.local/share/aerie/lockscreen/` and set as COSMIC's wallpaper (COSMIC's lock and login screens show that picture). Restore, delete-data and uninstall put your original wallpaper back.
- **Sound:** all sounds are generated on your computer; Aerie never records or listens to audio.
- **Cookies:** none. Aerie uses no cookies or tracking storage, so there is no cookie banner.
- **Children:** Aerie collects no personal data from anyone, including children. The assistant's Claude and ChatGPT options require an account with those companies, which set their own age rules; children should use Ollama or no assistant.
- **Moved from Deskscape:** on first start Aerie copies your Deskscape setup and files from `~/.config/deskscape` and `~/.local/share/deskscape` into its own folders. The old folders are left alone; you can delete them.
- **Deleting your data:** Settings → **Delete all my Aerie data** removes your setup, files you added, saved API keys and the autostart entry, and restores your original COSMIC look. Settings → **Uninstall Aerie** removes the app and all of that data in one step (and the update source, if one was added).
- **Links and web search:** Quick links, the search widget's optional web search, and 3D objects set to open a website open them in your normal browser, whose own privacy policy applies.
